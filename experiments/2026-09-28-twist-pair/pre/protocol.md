# twist-corrected: folding protocol

Generated when this experiment was created. If you change anything,
change it in `during/log.md` as a deviation rather than editing here --
this file is what was *meant* to happen.

## Buffer

5.0 mM Tris, 1.0 mM EDTA, pH 8.0.

## Magnesium screen

Origami folding is sharply Mg-dependent, so this is a screen rather than
a value. One reaction each at, in mM:

  5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20

9 reactions of 50 ul. Scaffold p7249 at 10 nM, each staple at 50 nM.

## Anneal

1. Hold at 65.0 C for 15 min.
2. Ramp 60.0 to 40.0 C at 1 C/min.
3. Total 0.58 h.

## Purification

  agarose gel extraction / PEG precipitation / 100 kDa spin filters

## Record for every reaction

Into `during/log.md`. A reaction whose conditions were not recorded
cannot be compared with one whose were.

- MgCl2 used
- gel: leading band position and smearing
- yield estimate
- which reaction went on to imaging

these are starting points from the literature, not results. The Mg2+ optimum moves with the design, which is why this is a series and not a number. Record what was actually used with every downstream result: a prediction made at one salt is not comparable to a measurement at another.

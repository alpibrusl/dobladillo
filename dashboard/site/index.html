At the bench

Measure the tip. Before and after.

A half-height footprint is wider than the object by twice √(R·h − h²/4). A nominal 10 nm tip is routinely 8–20 nm in use, and the correction goes as √R, so the tooling refuses to run without a measured value rather than substituting the nominal one.

Commit pre/ before the session

The comparison will reconcile a measurement with whatever is predicted. A prediction adjusted after seeing a scan produces output identical to one made in advance, so the distinction lives in the record and the tooling checks the timestamps.

The provenance column names the design the prediction is of and the design the oligos were ordered from, by content hash. experiment report refuses when they differ: a prediction tested against a different object is not a test.

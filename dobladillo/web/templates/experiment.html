How an experiment is kept
Plan pre/
Everything fixed before the experiment: the plan, the pass/fail criterion, what the software predicts, what to order and the protocol. Locking it records, with a date, that it was written before any data existed.
Lab record during/
What happened at the bench: the session log, the instrument's settings and the raw files exactly as the instrument wrote them.
Results post/
Computed by the software from the two above. Nothing there is edited by hand.
Pass/fail criterion
Written in advance: the result that would count as a failure. Afterwards it is shown next to the results; deciding the verdict is yours.
